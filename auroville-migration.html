the ask

After the ClearTax migration this one was easier, but the firm wanted only Books, with a way to manage many entities: a parent Books org with multiple child orgs. They are accountants simplifying their bookkeeping, not an ERP case, so Zoho Practice with subsidiary handling was the answer. That meant introducing hierarchy.

what we built

  1. HierarchyA parent Books org with multiple child orgs, managed from Zoho Practice.
  2. Bulk propagationPick a chart of accounts once and it is added to all the child orgs in bulk.
  3. Entity lockStops selected users from creating entities without permission. It is configurable from Zoho Practice.
  4. Client report builderLets the firm see and compare reports across clients by summing the selected child clients' reports.

My manager and I decided we needed the client report builder, bulk propagation and entity lock. I then worked on the implementations with the Practice team and joined meetings with the Auroville-facing team to hear their hardships and build solutions and customisations for them.
