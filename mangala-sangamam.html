origin story

My grandfather spent his free time collecting photos and details of marriage candidates in our community and sharing them free of cost, all in pen-and-paper notebooks. I built this app on Firebase to take his community matrimony off paper.

timeline

  1. Before the appNotebooksPhotos and details collected by hand and shared free of cost.
  2. 2020Python desktop GUIThe first digital version of the idea.
  3. March 2022Flutter mobile appThe shipped version, with profiles in a Firebase database.
  4. 2023300+ usersActive in the community.
