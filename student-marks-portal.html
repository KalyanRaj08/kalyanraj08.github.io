origin story

My uncle, a professor at a government college, kept student marks in Excel sheets and often lost track of them. I built him a tool that reads a CSV and stores the data the way he wanted.

what it does

  1. UploadA friendly interface accepts one or more CSV files. For each CO division you type the question numbers, comma separated, and put "-" on the divisions you do not need.
  2. StoreThe data is added to a database inside Django.
  3. DownloadWhen needed, the whole database comes back as a .csv or a .sqlite file.
  4. ClearOne button deletes all the data in the database.
