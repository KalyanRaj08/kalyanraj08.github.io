My interest in coding started in 9th grade, thanks to my Computer Science teacher. Learning Python Turtle fascinated me. The idea that a few lines of code could create something on screen felt almost magical. I later grew attached to C and Turbo C++, where coding felt like turning my thoughts directly into something I could see.

After school, I wanted to pursue Computer Science, but people around me convinced me it had limited opportunities. So I chose my second passion, Robotics. I loved the idea of combining coding with hardware, programming an Arduino, building things with my hands, and maybe getting enough movement to avoid becoming a lazy, fat guy sitting at a desk all day.

Even while studying Mechanical Engineering, I never stopped coding. Beyond my classes, I taught myself Python, Java, Flutter, Django, GUI development, and more. During COVID, I even joined programming courses meant for my CS and ECE friends using their accounts. Thankfully, online classes didn’t make it too obvious who was attending!

Eventually, I realized that software was where my passion truly belonged. Today, I’m an Application Developer at Zoho, working with Java, MySQL, REST APIs, backend systems, and enterprise applications.

Mechanical Engineering may be my degree, but software has always been my passion.

And yes, I still do calisthenics and walk 3 km a day, just to make sure I don’t become the fat guy at the desk.
